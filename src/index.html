A Sanctuary in the Heart of the Jungle

When you think of the wilderness of Jim Corbett National Park , it's likely that you also imagine a river winding through dense forest—and a serene stay along its banks. A few decades ago, this very imagination sparked the idea of creating riverside resorts. Today, that vision has come to life, with a myriad of options lining the banks of the Kosi and Ramganga rivers, perfectly complementing the dreams of every traveler seeking nature's embrace. Experience the perfect blend of wilderness and comfort at a riverside resort near Corbett National Park , where nature meets serenity.

Imagine sipping a hot cup of tea or coffee while seated by the shimmering river, the soothing sound of flowing water adding to the tranquility. As evening falls, gather around a cozy campfire under the stars , making unforgettable memories with family or friends.

Thanks to its prime riverside location and proximity to key safari zones , riverside resorts are among the most sought-after stays near Jim Corbett National Park. Whether you're setting out on a thrilling 4×4 jeep safari through the park's dense jungles or hiking scenic waterfall trails , your riverside resort serves as the ideal base for exploration. Adding to the experience, professional naturalists at these resorts offer enrich enrich insights into the park's rich biodiversity, enhancing every moment of your jungle retreat. Stay close to nature, stay close to adventure — only at a riverside resort in Corbett

Luxury Resorts with World-Class Experiences

The luxury resorts in the Jim Corbett region, including Tarangi Ramganga Resort, Namah Corbett, Solluna Resort, Jim Corbett Marriott Resort, Corbett River View Retreat, and Corbett Hideaway, offer an excellent combination of luxury, comfort, nature, and wildlife experiences. These properties feature sprawling landscaped lawns, riverside decks, attractive restaurants, large swimming pools, beautifully appointed rooms with contemporary amenities, and spa and wellness facilities, providing guests with a comfortable and relaxing stay. One of their key strengths is their strategic location close to the natural surroundings of Corbett, with several properties offering scenic riverfront settings and easy access to major safari areas such as Dhikala and Bijrani. These resorts also have in-house naturalists with extensive knowledge of the flora and fauna of Corbett National Park, who enhance the guest experience by providing valuable insights into wildlife, birds, vegetation, and the park's ecosystem. Guests can also enjoy activities such as jungle safaris, nature walks, birdwatching, and other outdoor experiences. In addition, spacious lawns, banquet facilities, and outdoor venues make these resorts suitable for destination weddings, corporate events, conferences, family gatherings, and group tours. Although there are other luxury resorts in Corbett, such as Aahana Resort and The Bagh, the combination of strategic riverside locations, proximity to major safari zones, experienced naturalists, extensive resort facilities, luxury accommodation, and accessibility for large groups gives these properties a distinctive competitive advantage in the Jim Corbett hospitality market.

Riverside Retreats by the Water's Edge

Nestled along the serene riverbanks of Jim Corbett, properties like Bel-la Monde Riverside Resort, Tarangi Ramganga Resort, Corbett River Creek, and Camp Riverwild offer an authentic riverside retreat where guests can unwind right along the water's edge. Beyond expansive shorelines, these destinations stand out for their open-air dining and riverside grill houses, where visitors can savor meals accompanied by the soothing sounds of running water and panoramic views of the shimmering river. Adding to the charm, many properties feature accommodations positioned directly along the bank—most notably the riverfront rooms at Bel-la Monde Riverside Resort, which boast a uniquely immersive location. These rooms offer unobstructed vistas of the flowing riverbed right from private balconies and glass windows, combining luxurious indoor comfort with direct visual and auditory access to nature.

Hotel Ratings & HRACC Classification

Important Note on Hotel Ratings and HRACC Classification

The Hotel and Restaurant Approval and Classification Committee (HRACC) is a central body under the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, responsible for the official inspection, approval, and classification of hotels and heritage properties under the prescribed standards, ranging from 1-Star to 5-Star Deluxe.

Please note that a hotel or resort describing itself as "5-Star Luxury", or receiving a 5-star rating on booking, review, or other online platforms, does not necessarily mean that it holds an official 5-Star HRACC classification.

The HRACC classification is an official government classification granted through a formal application, inspection, and evaluation process based on the standards prescribed by the Ministry of Tourism.

Disclaimer Regarding Ratings on Our Website

The hotel ratings displayed on our website are based on the ratings or classifications stated by the respective hotels on their official websites, Google listings, or other publicly available sources. These ratings are provided for informational and comparison purposes only.

They should not be interpreted as official HRACC classifications unless specifically stated and verified as such.

For the most accurate and up-to-date information regarding a hotel's official classification, we recommend referring to the Ministry of Tourism/HRACC's official records or confirming the classification directly with the property.
