Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an enquiry form, we may collect the following personal information:

Identity Data: Your full name and country of residence.

Contact Data: Your email address and phone number.

Travel Preferences: Destination interests, number of travelers, and planned travel dates.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for professional travel services, including:

Providing personalized tour quotes and travel itineraries tailored to your preferences and requirements.

Facilitating resort bookings, night stay booking in forest rest houses, and National Park safari permits on your behalf.

Communicating essential updates regarding your travel arrangements, including confirmations, reminders, and any changes.

Internal record-keeping to improve our guest services and ensure continuity of care for returning visitors.

Data Sharing & Operational Transparency

To fulfill your travel requests and ensure a seamless journey, we share specific data with our trusted service partners:

Resorts & Hotels: We share your Name and Contact Number with the property to confirm your room reservation and ensure their hospitality team is prepared for your arrival.

National Park Safari Bookings: For the processing of official government safari permits, we are required to share your Name, Age, and Identification (ID) Number with the official safari booking portal of Corbett National Park.

Service Guarantee: We only share the minimum amount of data required for these official purposes. Your information is never shared beyond what is necessary to complete your booking.

Legal Ownership: All such data processing is conducted under the legal management of Green View Voyage Pvt. Ltd. (CIN Number U63040DL2010PTC201816).

No Sale of Data

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. We also do not participate in repeated WhatsApp or email advertising; all contact remains strictly focused on your specific travel needs and inquiries.

Your trust is paramount to us, and we are committed to maintaining the confidentiality of your personal information at all times.

Cookies and Analytics

Our websites use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze site traffic via Google Analytics. These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website, allowing us to improve our content and services.

You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings. Please note that declining cookies may affect the functionality of certain features on our website.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you or to ask for your data to be corrected or deleted. We respect your right to control your personal information.

For any privacy-related inquiries, data access requests, or concerns, please contact us at tours@theindianvoyage.com. We aim to respond to all requests within 48 business hours.

Independent Status Disclosure

As an independent travel agency, we collect this data to provide private consultancy and booking services. We are not an official government body, and your data is managed entirely within our private corporate framework (Green View Voyage Pvt. Ltd.).

This independence allows us to offer unbiased recommendations and competitive pricing across multiple properties, always prioritizing your best interests.

Our Commitment to You

Your privacy and trust are the foundation of our business. We are committed to transparency, data security, and ensuring your travel experience is exceptional from the first inquiry to your journey home.

Questions? Contact us at tours@theindianvoyage.com
