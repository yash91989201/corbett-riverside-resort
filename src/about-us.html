About Us

Our Mission & Destination Specialization

Welcome to CorbettRiversideResort.com — your premier guide to the wilderness of Jim Corbett National Park. As a specialized portal under our flagship brand, The Indian Voyage, we are dedicated to providing the most in-depth expertise, curated itineraries, and seamless booking experiences.

We believe that every destination deserves its own dedicated platform to ensure travelers have access to the deep local knowledge required for an unforgettable journey. Our commitment to Jim Corbett's riverside resorts means you get specialized, insider knowledge that general travel sites simply cannot match.

A Decade of Excellence & Guest Trust

For over 15 years, our team of wildlife experts and travel consultants has helped more than 10,000 guests explore the natural heritage of India. Our commitment to quality is reflected in our consistent 5-star ratings on Google and TripAdvisor over the last decade.
